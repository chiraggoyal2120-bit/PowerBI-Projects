# Tata Data Visualisation: Empowering Business with Effective Insights 📊

## 📌 Project Overview
This repository contains my work for the **Tata Data Visualisation Job Simulation** hosted on Forage. As a PGDM candidate specializing Business Analytics at I Business Institute, my objective with this project was to bridge the gap between raw datasets and executive decision-making. 

Rather than just focusing on the mechanics of building a dashboard, this simulation pressure-tested my ability to think like a strategic consultant—framing the right questions, establishing visual hierarchy, and transforming complex data into a clear narrative that empowers business leadership to act.

## 🎯 Business Objective
The core goal of this simulation was to anticipate stakeholder needs and design visuals that explicitly drive informed business decisions. 

**Key areas of focus included:**
*   **Framing the Business Context:** Defining the exact metrics management needs to see to evaluate operational performance and revenue drivers.
*   **Strategic Visualization:** Moving away from default charts to select formats that clearly expose bottlenecks, trends, and opportunities.
*   **Actionable Insights:** Ensuring the final output is not just a data dump, but a clean, executive-ready narrative.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
*   **Power BI:** For end-to-end dashboard design and visual storytelling.
*   **Power Query:** For robust data cleaning, transformation, and ETL processes.
*   **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions):** To create dynamic calculated measures for deeper, multi-layered insights.
*   **Data Modeling:** Structuring relationships to ensure the underlying data architecture is highly efficient.

## 💡 Key Takeaways
1. **Visual Hierarchy matters:** The most critical KPIs must demand attention first. If an executive has to search for the answer, the dashboard has failed.
2. **Context is everything:** A chart without a clear business purpose is just a graphic. Every visual must tie directly to a potential business decision.
3. **Keep it actionable:** Translating multi-variable findings into plain English is the most valuable skill a data professional can have.
